        Does it really measure CT and systems thinking? Open questions

        • Every metric above is a behavioral proxy. None is shown to be CT or systems thinking until it is checked against something outside the game.
        • Clearing a level can come from blind trial and error. Compare runs-to-clear with one-change edits and the prediction score before reading a clearing rule as a sign of reasoning.
        • The prediction has two to five choices, so guessing succeeds often, especially at level 1. Use think-aloud to see whether players reason from arrivals and green time.
        • Content check: ask two or three researchers to map each metric to a CT or systems-thinking definition and mark the ones that do not fit.
        • Convergent check: compare scores with a validated CT test, Bebras-style items, or a stock-and-flow task (for example, a bathtub-dynamics item) from the same players.
        • Transfer: levels 4 and 5 share structure. A rule that clears level 4 often clears level 5 too, so "fewer runs" may reflect reuse rather than new understanding. Log the first rule tried at level 5 to tell them apart.
        • Level 6 asks a yes/no gridlock question, so guessing is right half the time. The more telling signal is whether gridlocked runs stop happening once the player has seen one.
        • Confounds to rule out: prior traffic-game experience, reading load in the rule builder, and players who wait for the debrief instead of predicting.
