    Does it really measure CT? Open questions

    • Every metric above is a behavioral proxy. None is shown to be CT until it is checked against something outside the game.
    • Content check: ask two or three CT researchers to map each metric to a CT definition and mark the ones that do not fit.
    • Convergent check: compare gate scores with a validated CT test or Bebras-style items from the same players.
    • Think-aloud check: watch five players and confirm that "one change at a time" reflects intent, not luck.
    • Reliability: gates 3 and 5 have the same logic, so a player's tests should be similar on both. Large gaps point to feature difficulty or noise.
    • Systems-thinking check: the road check has three choices, so guessing succeeds one time in three. Use think-aloud to see whether players reason from the rule, and compare with an outside systems-thinking measure.
    • Confounds to rule out: lucky guesses, prior Mastermind-style experience, emoji reading, prior route-planning experience, and attention.
